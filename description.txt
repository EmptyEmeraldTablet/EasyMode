Enemy Threat Slowdown
=====================

Slows hostile enemies and their projectiles that threaten the player.

Features:
- Enemy and boss movement slowdown
- Enemy projectiles/tears/rock waves slowdown
- Bomb explosion delay
- Player, friendly units, and familiars unaffected
- Configurable via config.lua and MCM

Factor range: 0-1 = slowdown, >1 = speed up (recommended up to 5x).
